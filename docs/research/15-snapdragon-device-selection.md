## 1. Summary & recommendation

1. **Gate on a Device Support Contract (DSC), not on the SoC.** Applying it to today's US market removes most "very high grade" Snapdragon phones: OnePlus 13/15 (newer bootloaders reject custom AVB keys; OnePlus is leaving the US), Galaxy S25/S26 (OEM unlock removed in One UI 8), ROG (ASUS stopped making phones [CORRECTED: ASUS's chairman said in Jan 2026 it "will no longer add new mobile phone models"; existing models keep software support, and ASUS did not rule out a return; OnePlus's US/EU exit was officially confirmed 2026-07-16 (new rollouts end; existing updates and warranty continue; OxygenOS moves to ColorOS from Android 17), per search summaries of 9to5google/Android Authority, not just the MacRumors "report" cited]), Xiaomi/Honor/Oppo/Vivo (no US carrier certification), Xperia 1 VII (US variant reportedly not unlockable; $1.3k+).
2. **What passes or probably passes:** Fairphone Gen 6+/6 (7s), Nothing Phone (3) (8s Gen 4) and (4a) Pro (7 Gen 4), Pixel 10a (reference, not Snapdragon), and Motorola Signature 27 (Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, GrapheneOS-confirmed, not yet shipping) to watch. **[CORRECTED] (second pass, 2026-10-02) "passes" is too strong: no Snapdragon device here has met the DSC MUST list. The only relock evidence is user reports in avbroot issue 299, and that list covers Fairphone 3/4/5 (not 6 or 6+), Nothing Phone (1)/(2)/(3)/(3a)/(4a) (not (4a) Pro) and Sony up to Xperia 1 VI (not 1 VII). Only the Pixel 10a has vendor-documented custom-key relock. Treat every Snapdragon row as "candidate pending bring-up".**
3. **BSP route (the crux): OEM-partnered retail-flash.** Our AOSP 17 system on the OEM's own vendor/firmware partitions, re-signed with our AVB key and relocked via `avb_custom_key`. LineageOS device trees are the engineering starting point. There is no adevtool equivalent for Snapdragon: adevtool is Pixel-only [PRIMARY]. Own-design ODM is a gated Stage 3.
4. **Picks.** v0 dev: Cuttlefish + 2 Pixel 10a/9a (keep) + 2 Fairphone 6 + 1 Nothing Phone (3). Stage-1 launch device: **Fairphone Gen 6+** (conditional on OEM terms and a passing relock test); second source: Pixel 10a. Premium/AI-tier candidates for Stage 2: Nothing Phone (3), then Signature 27 after it ships.
5. **Price:** about $650-900 hardware-bundle tier, not the $100-240 incumbent tier; needs a subscription/financing model (decision 3). **[CORRECTED] This item and decision 3 predate D15/D16 in docs/REQUIREMENTS.md: the founder's model is "sell the OS image; customers bring a qualified phone; we do not sell or inventory hardware" (D15), company-flashed in v1 (D16). The $649-899 figure is therefore the customer's cost of a qualified phone, not our bundle price, and the "$899 bundle / $30 x 24 financing / landed cost / returns" arithmetic in decision 3 does not apply. What D15/D16 add instead: eligibility checks on customer-supplied units (exact model/SKU, carrier-unlocked, OEM-unlock available, no carrier or financing lock, firmware new enough), handling of customer property, and the fact that OEM firmware is already on the phone (so the redistribution exposure in risk 1 may shrink, but a vbmeta chain over the customer's own OEM partitions still has to be generated and OEM updates still have to be ingested).**
6. **Report 02:** keep Pixel as reference/fallback (D13 says "not required", not "forbidden"); supersede its "Pixel-first v1" and "no voice/SMS" lines.

## 2. Findings

### F1. Device Support Contract (DSC)
MUST: AVB custom-key relock reaching a stable locked state; OEM unlock can be disabled after provisioning; A/B; AIDL HALs and VINTF; hardware-backed keystore (TEE ok); fastboot-flashable; 8 GB+ RAM; US T-Mobile/AT&T VoLTE+VoWiFi certification; OEM written permission to redistribute its firmware inside our OTA and to pre-flash.
SHOULD: 5+ years of security updates; GKI kernel; StrongBox; MTE; physical SIM + eSIM; drop-tolerant (no folds/glass slabs); price <= $700.
Benchmark: GrapheneOS's own list requires monthly patches within a week, 5+ years, GKI, MTE, StrongBox via secure element, and says it will "partner with OEMs" rather than lower the bar [PRIMARY] GrapheneOS/grapheneos.org@main:static/faq.html. Zune's bar is lower (no MTE/StrongBox needed).

### F2. Custom-key relock is OEM-specific and mostly undocumented

So "documented only for Pixel" is true of that one file, not evidence that other OEMs lack the feature (the avbroot matrix below shows several have it). This is a LineageOS fork of external/avb; Google's own tree was not reachable (403), so it is a mirror, not an AOSP primary.**
- User-reported matrix: works on Fairphone 3/4/5, Nothing Phone (1)/(2)/(3)/(3a)/(4a), Sony Xperia 1 II/1 V/1 VI/10 V, Motorola g-series and ThinkPhone, Razer Phone 2; **fails on newer OnePlus (boot failure or brick), Nothing (2a), all Xiaomi/Poco/Redmi**; Samsung not listed [SECONDARY] https://github.com/chenxiaolong/avbroot/issues/299 . OEMs can remove support in a later bootloader (OnePlus precedent) [INFERRED].
- **[CORRECTED] Matrix coverage: issue 299 lists "works" for Fairphone 3, 4, 5 (no Fairphone 6/6+), Nothing Phone (1)/(2)/(3)/(3a)/(4a) (not the (4a) Pro, which is the only 4a sold in the US), Sony Xperia 1 II/1 V/1 VI/5 IV/10 V (no Xperia 1 VII), Motorola g-series/Edge S/ThinkPhone, OnePlus 6/6T, Razer Phone 2; "no support": newer OnePlus (boot failure/brick), Nothing (2a), Xiaomi tested models (for Xiaomi the wording is "custom keys accepted but not enforced", a security failure rather than a refusal). - Samsung removed the OEM-unlock toggle from One UI 8 in all regions; the Knox fuse trips on unlock [SECONDARY] https://xdaforums.com/t/oem-unlock-on-galaxy-s26-series-one-ui-8-5-is-it-still-possible.4781825/ . **[CORRECTED] That XDA link is a question thread ("is it still possible?"). ### F4. BSP and support-window facts
- **What a Snapdragon device tree contains** (OnePlus 13, lineage-24.0, commit 2026-09-20): GKI kernel 6.6.142, about 15 out-of-tree `qcom/opensource/*` kernel modules (audio, camera, dsp, eva, graphics, securemsm), and a **1,009-line proprietary-files list** extracted from stock firmware; its AVB args disable verification (LineageOS-style builds are not verified-boot products) [PRIMARY] LineageOS/android_device_oneplus_sm8750-common@lineage-24.0; LineageOS/android_kernel_oneplus_sm8750@lineage-24.0:Makefile. **[CORRECTED] Counts re-measured 2026-10-02. OnePlus 13 common (HEAD 2026-09-29): kernel 6.6.142 confirmed, but proprietary-files.txt has 1,009 lines of which 863 are blob entries (rest comments/blank) and BoardConfigCommon lists 29 `qcom/opensource` module entries (about 20 distinct source trees), not "about 15". The Fairphone FP6 tree (lineage-24.0, HEAD 2026-09-30), the report's actual Stage-1/v0 family, differs: kernel **6.1.177** (GKI, `BOARD_USES_GENERIC_KERNEL_IMAGE := true`, SM7635), **1,478 blob entries** plus 24 firmware entries, 31 `qcom/opensource` entries (the page-size question below stays open: `BOARD_KERNEL_PAGESIZE := 4096` is only the boot-image header field, not proof of a 4 KB-only kernel). Say "GKI 6.1-6.6, 25-30 Qualcomm module entries, 860-1,500 blobs" and do not generalise the OnePlus numbers. The FP6 tree also keeps `system_dlkm` and `init_boot` in its partition list, and its AVB setup passes `--flags 3` (verification off) unless WITH_AVB=true.**
