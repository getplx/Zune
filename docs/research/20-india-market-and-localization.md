## 4. Recommended Stage-1 design

**4.1 Pre-flash checks (add to report 18):** GST invoice and serial; IMEI via KYM; reject grey and US-SKU units; read OEM-unlock and `get_unlock_ability` on the stock OS online; bootloader version vs bundle; Google account and FRP removed; battery health; nano-SIM tray present.

**4.2 Telephony:** keep stock Telecom, IMS and CarrierSettings; **reject incoming calls, drop SMS/MMS, block USSD, allow only 112** [INFERRED; prototype `DISALLOW_OUTGOING_CALLS`/`DISALLOW_SMS` plus a no-dialer Telecom first, falling back to report 12's Telecom patch as a constant policy]. A minimal Emergency screen dials 112 and shows 1098, 1930, 14416 as read-aloud cards, reachable by power-button press. **[CORRECTED]** (a) the child's device cannot dial 1098, 1930 or 14416 (deny-all except 112, A9), so the assistant's crisis scripts and these cards must say "tell a grown-up or press the SOS button", never "call 14416"; (b) replace 1930 (financial-fraud line) with the cybercrime.gov.in portal for a parent, and keep 112 as the child's only dial; (c) enforce "112 only" in our own screen, because the platform emergency list is radio-supplied (section 2.2). Residual bypass the report omits: the SIM is a live KYC'd voice SIM, so a child (or anyone) can move it to any other phone and get unfiltered calls, SMS and a browser; the parent contract and pairing flow must say so, and a data-only SIM, if one exists, is the better product.
